FG Cuts Petrol Price at NNPC Stations for 30 Days. NLC Fires Back With a Two-Week Ultimatum

By Trending9ja Staff • October 11, 2026 • 4 min read

Photo: THISDAY

On Thursday, 8 October, Finance Minister and Coordinating Minister of the Economy Taiwo Oyedele unveiled a 10-point package aimed at easing petrol prices. Headline measures: a 30-day discount on petrol at NNPC stations nationwide, with priority for public transporters, and a negotiated ceiling of N1,350 per litre on the ex-gantry price, reviewed monthly.

Other items include a National Strategic Fuel Reserve, faster CNG rollout, removal of illegal levies, forward sales of crude to domestic refineries and a possible excess profit tax on operators. Oyedele insists it is neither a subsidy nor price control: "Pump prices should not have to follow every swing in global crude or the exchange rate." He blamed a Middle East conflict now in its eighth month, with Brent above $100 a barrel.

Labour isn't impressed. The NLC, in a communique signed by president Joe Ajaero, gave government two weeks from 9 October to return petrol to 2024 levels, and wants minimum wage talks to begin before the end of October.

The opposition is also swinging. Atiku Abubakar called the discount temporary and election-driven. The ADC campaign council called it a pre-election bribe: "Nigerians will reject your one-month bribe." Presidential aide Bayo Onanuga has dismissed reports of a returning subsidy as fake news.

ADC Launches Atiku–Amaechi Campaign: "Tinubu Made Life Expensive, I Will Make Life Affordable"

By Trending9ja Staff • October 11, 2026 • 4 min read

Photo: Vanguard

The African Democratic Congress (ADC) has formally kicked off its 2027 presidential campaign, unveiling its Presidential Campaign Council in Abuja on Friday, 9 October. The party's ticket is Atiku Abubakar and Rotimi Amaechi.

Speaking at the event, the campaign's Director-General, Austin Akobundu, quoted Atiku directly: "Tinubu made life expensive. I will make life affordable again." Campaign Council Chairman Kashim Ibrahim-Imam went harder: "The palace feasts while the people fast!" and "Tinubu priced you out of your own country. Atiku will bring you back in."

The party also pointed to public debt, which it says rose from N87.38 trillion when Tinubu took office to N166.79 trillion by 30 June 2026. These are the ADC's figures, as cited at the launch.

National Chairman David Mark and the campaign team accused the administration of using state agencies to intimidate party members, vowing: "The ADC will resist every form of intimidation and harassment!" They also condemned the continued detention of ADC Deputy Chairman Nasir El-Rufai, a former Kaduna State governor.

Photo: Ripples Nigeria

A total of 51,000 viewers, counting only those who stayed from start to finish, qualified for the record at Tafawa Balewa Square (TBS) in Lagos.

Far-Right AfD Wins About 44% in Saxony-Anhalt, Its Biggest Win Yet, Leaving Merz Under Pressure

By Trending9ja Staff • October 11, 2026 • 4 min read

Photo: Kuwait Times / AFP

Germany's far-right Alternative for Germany (AfD) won about 44% of the vote in the Saxony-Anhalt state election on Sunday, 6 September, its biggest win yet, according to broadcaster projections reported by AFP. Chancellor Friedrich Merz's CDU took about 18%.

The AfD fell three seats short of the 42 needed for an absolute majority, which could mean lengthy coalition talks in Magdeburg, and the leftist BSW could play kingmaker if it clears the 5% threshold. Germany's domestic intelligence agency classifies the AfD's state branch as "right-wing extremist."

CDU figures were blunt. Schleswig-Holstein premier Daniel Guenther called it "a shocking moment for our country," while Dennis Radtke, who chairs the party's employee wing, called it a "watershed moment." Political scientist Oliver Lembcke described "a tectonic shift in the party system."

AfD lead candidate Ulrich Siegmund called Merz "a very good campaigner" for his party, saying every day with Merz in the chancellery was "one day too many." The AfD sees the result as a blueprint for the 2029 national elections.

FXStreet's October outlook notes the AfD also won a second eastern state election in September, describing it as a major political setback to Merz.

As Russia Escalates, the EU Admits Its Ukraine Support "Won't Be Enough"

By Trending9ja Staff • October 11, 2026 • 4 min read

Photo: Euronews

Russia's escalation has raised doubts about the EU's support programmes for Ukraine. The €90 billion loan agreed last year covers two-thirds of Ukraine's needs for 2026 and 2027, but it was designed on the assumption that the war would end this year.

Ukraine's Finance Minister Sergii Marchenko warned that the 2027 budget will be the most complicated since the full-scale invasion, citing a $78 billion (€70 billion) gap. Economy Commissioner Valdis Dombrovskis admitted: "It's clear that we are facing a sizeable funding gap." The Commission plans to provide €45 billion next year.
